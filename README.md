## The Niche (why now)

**Invisible AI watermarking / "spymarks" — the loudest creator-privacy story of September 2026.**

The signal was overwhelming and consistent across every source I could reach. This is not a trend guess; it's a live news cycle colliding with a genuine creator pain point.

**News cycle (Google News / Bing News RSS, Sept 2026):**
- *"Spymarks, Not Watermarks"* — HN front page **442 points** (Sept 21), the article that named the problem
- Gizmodo: *"People Are Rushing to Find Ways to Remove Claude's AI Text Watermark"*
- WIRED: *"Coders Say They Already Found Workarounds to Claude's Invisible Watermarks"*
- TechCrunch: *"Anthropic says it will watermark text generated by its AI models"*
- The Register: *"Microsoft AI watermarks in Paint and Photos are linked to user IDs, researcher finds"*
- Cybernews: *"Lies And Scams Taint Watermark Removal Apps Now That Anthropic Started Watermarking Claude AI Output"*
- IEEE Spectrum: *"AI Models Are Watermarking Text — Will You Notice?"*
- HN (332 pts): *"OpenAI Adopts Google's SynthID Watermark for AI Images"*
- HN (387 pts): *"Remove-AI-Watermarks — CLI and library for removing AI watermarks from images"*

**The core insight:** a *watermark* verifies authenticity; a **spymark** is a hidden signal that makes your work traceable *without your knowledge or consent*. Google's SynthID can encode a **136-bit payload** in a 512×512 image — enough for a 64-bit database ID linked to a person. Creators just discovered they can no longer be told apart from machines — and worse, their files are leaking identities they never agreed to share.

**The gap:** there is no cheap, credible, *non-scammy* system that helps an honest creator (a) find what's being written into their files, (b) strip covert tracking, (c) replace it with their own proof of authorship, and (d) charge a premium for the difference. The existing market is either scam removers or free command-line tools with no system around them.

> *Note: `web_search` was disabled this run, and no YouTube/Zernio trend API was available (Zernio is a publishing API with no trending endpoint). Trend research was done via Hacker News (Algolia front page + best page), Google News RSS, Bing News RSS, Google Trends RSS, and Product Hunt. HN + news RSS were the strongest and most consistent signals.*
