## The Starter Block — your first three moves, tonight

Don't wait for day 1. Do these three things in the next 30 minutes:

1. **Sweep one file.** Open your most recent published image or doc. Run
   `exiftool -a -G1 -s file` (or view its properties). See what it's saying about you.
2. **Empty its container.** `exiftool -all= file`, then re-export from your editor.
3. **Log it.** Write your first Origin Record entry using the template in deliverable 2.
   Title, date, tools, human work. One entry. That's the beginning of your track record.

Thirty minutes from now, you'll own something you didn't own before: **a defensible file.**
