### Compliance rate (the number that counts)

```
Compliance % = (files with origin record) ÷ (files published) × 100
```

- **Target:** 100%. You're not publishing files without a record anymore.
- **Below 90%:** check where the gap is — usually a "quick post" that skipped the gate.

## Part 2 — The 30-Day Trust Score

Score each pillar **0–10** on day 30. Total out of 50.

### Pillar 1 — Detection habits (0–10)
Do you audit before publishing, or after someone asks?
- 10 = never published an unaudited file this month
- 5 = audited half of them
- 0 = never ran a metadata sweep

**Score: ___ /10**

### Pillar 2 — Clean files (0–10)
Are your published files actually clean?
- 10 = every file has an empty container + your own signature
- 5 = most files clean, a few old ones unaddressed
- 0 = published files still full of tool/device signatures

**Score: ___ /10**

### Pillar 3 — Sealed work (0–10)
Does your work carry *your* proof?
- 10 = badge + origin record on every piece
- 5 = inconsistent
- 0 = no seal at all

**Score: ___ /10**

### Pillar 4 — Public claim (0–10)
Can a stranger find your claim?
- 10 = bio + footer + quote script all in place
- 5 = one of the three
- 0 = nowhere

**Score: ___ /10**

### Pillar 5 — Monetized (0–10)
Is the trust premium showing up in your income?
- 10 = closed at least one Certified/retainer deal
- 5 = quoted Certified at least once
- 0 = still charging the same rate as before

**Score: ___ /10**

### Your Trust Score: ___ /50

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **40–50** | 🏆 Proven origin. Charge the premium. |
| **25–39** | ⚙️ System working — tighten the gaps. |
| **10–24** | ⚠️ Half-protected. The unfixed half is your risk. |
| **0–9** | 🚨 Publishing exposed. Start with Gate 1 today. |

## Part 5 — The compounding effect

Here's why the tracker matters. Provenance is not a one-time activity — it's a **stock**, not a flow. Every clean, sealed file you publish adds to a visible body of *verifiable* work. After 90 days of 100% compliance, you have something no competitor can fabricate overnight: **a track record with receipts.**

That record is what lets you say, calmly, with a straight face:

> "Every piece I've published in the last quarter carries an origin record. You can check any of them."

No one argues with receipts.

**Measure weekly. Score monthly. Compound forever.**
