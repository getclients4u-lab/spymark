# The Trust Premium Pricing Sheet™

**Deliverable 4 · SpyMark Protection System™**

How to turn "I can prove my work is human-made" into money you actually charge.

---

## The premise in one line

When buyers cannot tell human work from machine work, they **discount everyone** — and the only way to escape the discount is to prove origin. Origin is the one feature a competitor's output **cannot copy.**

So don't apologize for provenance. **Price it.**

---

## Part 1 — The three tiers

Give every client the same clean choice. Anchoring does the selling.

| | **Standard** | **Certified** ⭐ | **Turnkey** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Your normal rate | **+20–35%** | **+50–70%** or retainer |
| **What's included** | The deliverable as usual | Deliverable + Origin Record + HumanBadge + disclosure block | Certified + full audit of their existing library + staff one-pager + monthly re-audit |
| **Their risk** | Unverified | Verified authorship | Verified + compliant |
| **Best for** | Low-budget one-offs | Most clients | Agencies, publishers, regulated brands |

**Say it like this:**

> "Standard is the work. Certified is the work *plus the paperwork that proves a human made it* — that's the +25%. Turnkey is when I set up the whole proof system across your library — that's a retainer."

Most clients pick Certified. Certified is where your margin lives.

---

## Part 2 — The three levers

You don't have to raise your headline rate to earn more. Pull these three levers instead.

### Lever 1 — The Provenance Add-On
Attach provenance to existing work as a **standalone line item**.
> "For +$75 I'll audit, strip, and seal the three files you're about to publish — with an origin record you can show your client."

Costs you 20 minutes. Sells for the price of an hour.

### Lever 2 — The Compliance Bundle
Sell to businesses that *must* disclose (EU AI Act pressure, publisher policies, regulated industries).
> "Your team needs to prove human review on AI-assisted content. I'll deliver a 3-page process doc, a compliant disclosure block, and a 30-minute training call. $450 flat."

Fixed scope, fixed price, no scope creep, high perceived value.

### Lever 3 — The Trust Retainer
Recurring beats one-off.
> "$199/month: I certify your published library, keep your origin records current, and re-audit monthly. You never have an unprovable piece live."

Ten retainers = a job you own.

## Part 3 — The Premium Script (word-for-word)

Use this when a client pushes back on price. Do **not** discount — *re-frame*.

**Client:** "That's more than I expected for the same deliverable."

**You:**
> "It's the same deliverable, plus something your other options can't give you: proof of origin. In a market where anyone can generate a thousand versions of this in a minute, the thing that makes your work *yours* is the record that a person made the decisions. I log the origin, seal it, and hand you the evidence. That's what the difference buys — and it's the reason you don't get undercut later."

**Client:** "Can't you just do it at the old price?"

**You:**
> "I can do the work at the old price — without the certification. But I'd be doing you a disservice, because the certification is the part that protects your investment. Let's do Certified; if the record never gets used, you've lost 25% on one project. If it does, it saves your reputation."

**Client:** "How do I know the seal is legit?"

**You:**
> "You don't take my word for it — you check it. Here's my origin record format, here's a published example, and every file carries my badge so you always know where it came from. Verifiable beats promised."

The move in all three: **never defend the price — educate on the risk they're buying insurance against.**

## Part 5 — What you're really selling

Not paper. Not a badge. You are selling **the end of the buyer's doubt.**

When a founder publishes an AI-assisted post and a customer accuses them of slop, the certified creator has a one-page origin record and a calm reply. The uncertified creator has an argument. That difference — *record vs. argument* — is worth a premium to anyone with a reputation on the line.

Price the peace of mind, not the PDF.
